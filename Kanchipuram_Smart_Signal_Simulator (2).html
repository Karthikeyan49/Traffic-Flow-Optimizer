4 · The numbers

Move time = how long one vehicle needs to pull away (from our observation).
RoadVehiclesMove time (s/veh) Fixed greenSmart greenCleared (smart)Left (fixed)
How it works: Smart green = vehicles × move time, kept at or above the minimum green (10 s), with no upper limit so a busy road gets all the green it needs to clear. Both signals run the same traffic so the test is fair. Average wait is estimated assuming vehicles arrive evenly through the cycle, plus extra for any left waiting. Fuel and pollution savings are not shown here because they need extra assumptions.

Data: counts first observed by our team at the Kanchipuram Main signal (next to the bus stand) during the evening peak, from a safe place off the road. A model for the HCL Jigsaw Grade 8 project — not a live traffic feed.
